Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Version 1.2 · DRAFT FOR OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW

What we collect

StudyGrounds uses Amazon Cognito for authentication and may process account identifiers, email address, names, and profile details. We store notes, uploaded documents and images, publication settings, contributor profile information, saved items, study history and annotations, helpful votes, feedback, moderation reports, and application-security records when you use these functions.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, deliver requested features, keep private content associated with your account, publish content you choose to make public, investigate safety reports, secure uploads, detect abuse, maintain application reliability, and respond to support requests.

Public and private content

Notes are private until you publish them. Published notes and attached materials are available to visitors; public attribution may include your first name and last initial and your chosen public profile information. Avoid including personal or confidential data in public documents. Unpublishing does not ensure copies already downloaded by others disappear.

Service providers and security

StudyGrounds uses Amazon Web Services services including Cognito, Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3, CloudFront, monitoring, and malware scanning. Technical logs may contain identifiers and request metadata. Access controls, encryption, and scanning reduce risks but no system is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

You can request permanent account deletion from Settings or email privacy@joinstudygrounds.com. You may request access to or a copy of your personal account data through the same email address; self-service full-account export is not currently available. The service queues account removal, including Cognito credentials, private and published notes, source-file objects, saved items, study activity, profile preferences, and user-associated feedback and vote records. Moderation reports may be retained in de-identified form where required for safety and legal handling. Previously downloaded public files cannot be recalled. Deletion is not necessarily immediate; DynamoDB recovery snapshots and technical logs may persist until their configured retention periods expire. Actual log, backup, and retention settings must be verified in AWS before specific periods can be promised.

Disclosure and user choices

Published content is disclosed to other users and visitors. Authorized service providers may process information to operate and secure StudyGrounds, and information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address abuse. The current repository contains no checkout, paid-membership, or advertising integration. We do not currently intend to sell personal information; any future advertising, analytics, or payment features would require a new review and updated disclosures before launch. You control what you publish and can use available settings and feedback tools for inquiries.

Children and location

StudyGrounds is intended for people aged 13 and older and does not knowingly permit accounts for children under 13. We record an age-eligibility confirmation when you accept our Terms; we do not request a date of birth in this flow. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can investigate. Further review of regional privacy requirements remains necessary before production release.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and display updated effective dates. Material changes may require additional notice or permissions. StudyGrounds is currently owned and operated by Jonathan Feagans, an individual operating as StudyGrounds. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@joinstudygrounds.com. For general support, contact support@joinstudygrounds.com. Before production: finalize the rights-request process, retention schedule, any required mailing address, and applicable regional disclosures.
