Reporting claimed infringement

If you believe content hosted on StudyGrounds infringes your copyright, provide a written notice containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature as the owner or authorized agent.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed, or a representative list for multiple works at one online site.
  3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and information reasonably sufficient for us to find it, such as the StudyGrounds note URL.
  4. Your name and contact details, including an email address and mailing address or telephone number as applicable.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the challenged use is not authorized by the rights holder, the rights holder's agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner of the exclusive right allegedly infringed.

Review and removal

We review appropriately submitted notices and may promptly remove or disable access to the material in question. We may notify the uploader, preserve relevant records, and take appropriate action. Removal may happen without advance warning. A notice does not guarantee that every claim is valid.

Counter-notifications

If your content was removed following a copyright notice and you believe the removal was mistaken or the content was misidentified, you may submit a written counter-notification containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and its prior StudyGrounds location.
  3. Your name, address, and telephone number.
  4. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed because of mistake or misidentification.
  5. A statement consenting to jurisdiction of the appropriate United States federal district court under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g), and agreeing to accept service of process from the original complainant or their agent.

Where statutory requirements apply, StudyGrounds may provide a compliant counter-notification to the original complainant. Restoration is ordinarily considered no sooner than 10 and no later than 14 business days after receipt of a valid counter-notification, unless we first receive notice that the complainant has filed a qualifying court action or other grounds prevent restoration.

Repeat infringement

We maintain a policy of restricting or terminating accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances, based on substantiated notices and relevant context. We may also act against abusive or fraudulent complaints. Knowingly submitting materially false claims or counter-notifications may have legal consequences.
