Community Guidelines

Version 1.0 · November 19, 2026

Share what you have the right to share

Upload your own notes or material you have permission to distribute. Do not share pirated textbooks, copyrighted slides without authorization, leaked tests, or other people's personal information.

Keep learning fair

Do not use StudyGrounds to cheat, distribute answer keys or exams unlawfully, or misrepresent others' work as your own.

Keep the community safe

No harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, spam, malware, scams, or attempts to bypass technical safeguards. Do not publish sensitive personal or confidential information.

Report problems

Use in-app reports or feedback to flag problematic content. Moderators may remove or restrict content and accounts when warranted. These guidelines supplement the Terms of Use.
