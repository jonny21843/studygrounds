Community publishing guidelines

Help other learners find useful, trustworthy materials:

  • Only publish notes and source files you have the right to share.
  • Remove personal, confidential, or sensitive information before publishing.
  • Choose an accurate title, subject, language, and description.
  • Do not post spam, harassment, malware, or misleading material.

Public notes can be reported and reviewed by moderators.
